Stack-Based Navigation Concept (LIFO: Last-In, First-Out)

In a web browser, navigation history is maintained using a Stack data structure. When you visit a new website, your current URL is pushed onto the history stack. When you click the Back button, the previous page is retrieved from the top of the stack and popped off, restoring your last viewed page.
